*Illustrative example with invented data.* An invented training college, its invented staff and invented students. The inputs the academic office supplies come first, then the packet prepared from them.

## The packet

**ONLINE CLASS SETUP PACKET: BUS 214 Small Business Bookkeeping, section 02**

For: Learning-platform administrator. Prepared from the approval note AC-2027-0143, the teacher assignment, the roster export (10 rows) and the authorized invitation list. Draft for the administrator's verification. Nothing has been created or sent.

**Class request:** Course name: Small Business Bookkeeping. Course code: BUS 214. Section: 02. Term: Spring 2027. Room: C112. Meeting pattern: Tuesday and Thursday, 18:00 to 19:30. Enrolment cap: 8. Platform template: Evening course standard. Approval: AC-2027-0143, dean's office, 2 November 2026.

**Responsible teacher:** Ms Ardell, T-0412. Confirmed: Yes, in the teacher assignment.

**Roster counts:** Export rows 10. Set aside as another section: 1. Rows for this section: 9. Enrolled: 7. Waitlisted: 1. Status blank: 1. Repeated rows (counted once): 0. Enrolled and held out of the invitation list: 3. Enrolled and listed: 4. Enrolled is within the cap of 8.

**Settings used:** "Enrolled" counts as enrolled (as supplied); a class over its cap is an exception (default, since no cap rule was supplied); who holds each answer is as supplied.

**People who can be invited (enrolled, on the authorized list, no open exception):** 5
- T-0412 Ms Ardell, teacher
- S2041 Quill, Imani
- S2044 Reddan, Tomas
- S2049 Ferrante, Lucia
- S2052 Pell, Owen

**Roster exceptions:** 6
1. Teacher or staff: T-0455 Mr Brandt, teaching assistant, Confirmed: No, and on the authorized list. Not listed.
2. Duplicate identity: S2063 and S2064 both read Wirth, Jonas, both Enrolled in section 02. The authorized list has S2063 only. Neither is listed.
3. Enrolled, not authorized: S2057 Nandakar, Priya. Not on the authorized list. Not listed.
4. Not enrolled, authorized: S2070 Haskett, Mira, status Waitlisted. Not listed.
5. Status unclear: S2075 Sollis, Ahmed, status blank. Not on the authorized list. Not listed.
6. Authorized, not on roster: S2090. No row in the export. Not listed.

**Set aside:** S2080 Bellamy, Noor is on the export but in BUS 214, section 01. Not part of this packet.

**For the administrator to verify:** (1) Ownership: the teacher named in the approval note, Ms Ardell, matches the confirmed row for T-0412. (2) Membership: 4 of the 7 enrolled students can be listed; the 3 held out are exceptions 2 and 3. (3) Template: Evening course standard, as named in the approval.

**Decisions for the administrator or the academic office:** 6
1. Will the department or academic lead confirm Mr Brandt's teaching assistant assignment?
2. Which ID is Jonas Wirth's: S2063 (the one authorized), S2064, or are they two people? The registrar holds the answer.
3. Is S2057 Nandakar, Priya to be added to the authorized list, given the roster shows the student as Enrolled? The office that authorizes invitations decides.
4. Is S2070 Haskett, Mira, Waitlisted, to stay on the authorized list? The office that authorizes invitations decides.
5. What is S2075 Sollis, Ahmed's enrolment status? The registrar holds the answer.
6. Is S2090 a roster omission or an error on the authorized list? The registrar and the authorizing office decide.

Every figure and name traces to the approval note, the teacher assignment, the roster export, the authorized list and the settings supplied. The packet creates no class, creates no account, invites nobody and changes no record.
